Tension Pneumothorax

1
Assess Triad: Respiratory distress, hypotension, absent breath sounds.
2
Action: 100% O2. Notify Trauma Team.
3
Decompress: 14G Needle at 5th ICS (Anterior Axillary Line).

Acute Coronary Syndrome

1
Assess: Chest pain, radiation to jaw/arm, diaphoresis.
2
ECG: Obtain 12-lead ECG within 10 minutes.
3
MONA: Morphine, Oxygen, Nitroglycerin, Aspirin (per MD).

Stroke (CVA) Protocol

1
FAST: Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech difficulty, Time to call.
2
Glucose: Check blood sugar immediately to rule out hypoglycemia.
3
CT Scan: Immediate non-contrast Head CT.

Sepsis (qSOFA)

1
Check: RR > 22, Altered Mentation, Systolic BP < 100.
2
Labs: Draw Lactate and Blood Cultures.
3
Fluids: 30mL/kg Crystalloid bolus for hypotension.

General Triage (ESI)

1
Level 1: Resuscitation (Immediate life-saving intervention).
2
Level 2: Emergent (High risk, confused, or severe pain).
3
Level 3: Urgent (Requires multiple resources).
